# LYCANTHROPE
### Play the Game: https://mouse-jacket.itch.io/werewolf-escape
## Created for DIGM-350-001 - SU 25-26 
Stranded in the woods with no hope of direction, a lost hiker falls through the roof of a cabin while attempting to get a grasp of her surroundings. As she gets to her feet, she's apprehended by a man chained to a wall, warning her to be gone from the cabin within five minutes. It's up to you to find a way to escape, by any means necessary!

LYCANTHROPE is a short horror-puzzle game, that traps the player in an escape room with a werewolf that slowly transforms over the course of 5 minutes. The player must use environmental exploration and observations skills to assemble a keycode, making sure to pay close attention to narrative details to obtain the best of 3 endings. 

## Features
* A series of short, environmental puzzles with an emphasis on observation
* A branching narrative with 3 endings
* 4 fully voice-acted cutscenes
* Completely randomized puzzle solutions - no two games are played the same
* Custom shader and environmental design
